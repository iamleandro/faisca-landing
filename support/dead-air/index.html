Dead Air: Ashmere

Support

Last updated: September 1, 2026

Dead Air: Ashmere is a narrative investigation game for mobile devices. Listen to recorded radio traffic, question people, collect evidence, choose three clues, and file your deduction.

Need help? Email support@faisca.gg, or open Dead Air → Settings → Feedback → Report a problem.

System requirements

Dead Air requires:

  • an iPhone running iOS 17 or later (Android will be available soon);
  • an internet connection for credits, purchases, account actions, recoveries, and case unlocks; and
  • audio output through your iPhone speaker, headphones, or another connected device.

The game is designed for portrait orientation. It does not monitor live radio or emergency-service communications; all case recordings are fictional and included with the game.

Getting started

The current release includes Case 001 — The Missing Package, an approximately 30–45 minute investigation at Hawthorne Investigations.

To solve the case:

  1. Open the case file from the agency screen.
  2. Visit the Radio Monitor and listen to transmissions across all five channels.
  3. Answer listening questions and collect evidence.
  4. Contact the people connected to the case.
  5. Choose three clues on the evidence board.
  6. Open the deduction file and submit your conclusion.

You can replay recordings and change playback speed between 1.0×, 0.75×, and 0.5×.

Case 002 may appear as a locked or newly unlocked file after Case 001 is resolved. Its playable content is not included in the current version.

Languages and learning assistance

Dead Air supports interfaces and case assistance in:

  • English;
  • Spanish;
  • Portuguese (Brazil);
  • Simplified Chinese; and
  • Japanese.

Change the interface or case-text language under Settings → Language.

Transcripts, vocabulary, and listening questions remain in English because understanding the case’s English recordings is part of the investigation. Definitions, contextual explanations, slower playback, captions, and replay controls are available to help.

If you find an incorrect translation, choose Report this translation where available, or open Settings → Feedback → Report a wrong translation. You may write your report in any language.

Audio troubleshooting

If you cannot hear a recording:

  1. Raise your iPhone’s media volume.
  2. Check whether audio is being sent to headphones, a Bluetooth device, AirPlay, or CarPlay.
  3. Pause and replay the transmission.
  4. Close and reopen Dead Air.
  5. Restart your iPhone if the issue continues.

If only one recording is affected, report the problem from Settings and identify the channel or transmission. The in-app report automatically includes useful technical details.

Transcripts and captions

A transcript is initially hidden. After attempting a recording, you can spend 1 credit to reveal its written transcript.

If Prefer captions after a listen is enabled under Settings → Playback, the text is also shown after you complete a recording. You can tap highlighted vocabulary for a simple definition and contextual meaning.

How credits work

Every new player starts with 3 free credits.

One credit is used when you:

  • reveal a hidden transcript;
  • choose an incorrect answer to a listening question;
  • submit an incorrect deduction; or
  • request a translation during an interview.

When your total balance drops below 3, free credits recover four hours later, bringing your balance back up to 3. For example, a balance of 1 receives 2 free credits.

Purchased credits are kept separately, never expire, and are not removed by the free recovery. Free credits are used before purchased credits.

You can continue listening and use previously unlocked investigation tools with no credits. Actions that spend credits require an internet connection.

Buying credits

Tap the Credits meter in the game header to open the store. Available packs contain 10, 30, or 50 credits. Prices are supplied by the App Store and may vary by storefront.

Before your first purchase, Dead Air requires Sign in with Apple. This protects your purchased credits so they remain associated with a recoverable account if you reinstall the app or change devices.

If a purchase is waiting for Ask to Buy approval, the credits arrive after the purchase is approved. If payment succeeds but delivery is still processing, you may close the app; delivery should finish automatically.

A purchase completed, but my credits are missing

  1. Confirm that your iPhone is online.
  2. Reopen Dead Air and allow it a moment to refresh the balance.
  3. Confirm that you are using the same Apple Account connected to your Dead Air player.
  4. Check whether the purchase is still awaiting Ask to Buy approval.

If the credits still do not appear, contact support@faisca.gg. Include the approximate purchase date, the size of the credit pack, your app version, and any message shown in the store. Do not send passwords, sign-in codes, or full payment details.

Consumable credit packs do not use a conventional “Restore Purchases” button. Their balance is recovered through the Dead Air account connected with Sign in with Apple.

Playing offline

Downloaded case content remains substantially playable offline. You can listen to included recordings and revisit content and tools that have already been unlocked.

The credit meter may display your last known balance while offline. The following actions require a connection:

  • spending credits;
  • claiming a free-credit recovery;
  • buying or delivering a credit pack;
  • unlocking a case;
  • changing account-level privacy choices; and
  • deleting an account.

If you see “You need a connection for this. Your progress is safe,” reconnect and try the action again.

Saving, resetting, and changing devices

Your current investigation progress and settings are saved automatically on your iPhone. Your credit balance and case unlocks are maintained separately by Dead Air’s account service.

Selecting Settings → Reset case progress clears the active investigation and returns it to the beginning. It does not refund credits already spent.

Detailed investigation progress is stored locally and may be lost if Dead Air is deleted or reinstalled. Signing in with Apple before purchasing makes your account, purchased credits, and account-level unlocks recoverable.

Refunds

App Store purchases and refund decisions are handled by Apple. Dead Air cannot approve or guarantee a refund.

If Apple approves a refund for a credit pack, unused credits from that purchase may be removed from your balance. A refund never removes more credits than remain from the refunded purchase.

Under Settings → Privacy, you may choose whether Dead Air can provide Apple with limited information about the refunded pack when you initiate a refund request. This setting is off by default and can be turned off again at any time.

Privacy

Read the Dead Air Privacy Policy for information about accounts, purchases, analytics, crash reports, privacy choices, data retention, and account deletion.

Usage analytics are off unless you choose to enable them under Settings → Privacy. Dead Air does not contain advertising or cross-app tracking.

Deleting your account and data

Open Settings → Account → Delete account and data.

If your account is connected to Sign in with Apple, Apple will ask you to verify your identity before deletion can finish.

Deleting your account permanently removes your account, credits, unlocked cases, and local progress. Purchased credits are lost and cannot be restored. This action cannot be undone.

If deletion fails, nothing is deleted. Check your connection and try again. If your account uses Sign in with Apple, verify with the same Apple Account connected to your Dead Air player.

Accessibility and comfort settings

Under Settings → Playback, you can:

  • prefer captions after completing a recording;
  • reduce radio motion; and
  • turn haptics on or off.

Dead Air also supports slower audio playback for recordings.

Reporting a problem

The quickest option is Settings → Feedback → Report a problem. Dead Air opens a pre-filled email containing:

  • the Dead Air version and build;
  • your iOS version and device model;
  • your interface and case-text languages; and
  • the active case and screen.

Add a description of what happened and send it from your mail app. If no mail app is configured, the report is copied to your clipboard so you can send it manually.

You can also email support@faisca.gg directly. Please include:

  • what you were trying to do;
  • what happened instead;
  • any error message displayed;
  • steps that reproduce the problem;
  • your iPhone model, iOS version, and Dead Air version; and
  • a screenshot or screen recording, if helpful.

Please do not include passwords, Apple verification codes, authentication tokens, or full payment information.
